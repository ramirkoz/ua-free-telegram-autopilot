# UA FREE Telegram Autopilot v0.1.0-rc58

RC58 is an editorial rebuild focused on visible behavior change rather than transport plumbing.

## Two genuinely different channels
- CTRL+UA now has a dedicated broad-audience science/technology selector and writer contract.
- ПРОДАНО! now requires a concrete marketing mechanism: activation, campaign creative, creator/community mechanic, gamification, PR stunt, experiential/OOH, viral social pattern or useful behavioral insight.
- Personnel changes, conference announcements, routine SDK/API/login updates and pure platform metrics no longer pass ПРОДАНО! merely because they are "marketing news".

## Reaction Learning 2.0
- 👍/👎 remain topic signals only.
- 🔥 remains style only. Premium 👍+🔥 and 👎+🔥 stay independent by design.
- Topic learning now adds semantic editorial facets on top of the old lexical similarity, so feedback generalizes from individual words to classes such as cybersecurity, science, consumer how-to, campaign creative or gamification.
- Old lexical similarity is retained at reduced weight for near-story continuity and temporary dislike suppression.

## Visible learning
- Editorial Memory now includes a "Що система зрозуміла з реакцій" summary.
- It shows categories admins want more/less of, audience-positive categories, style-fire count and timestamp of the freshest feedback.

## Writing
- The final writer once again receives the actual channel editorial profile.
- CTRL+UA is explicitly written for an intelligent general reader without specialist education: 2–3 short paragraphs, plain explanations, one idea per sentence, no buying-guide/manual voice.
- ПРОДАНО! starts from the mechanism, not CMO/API/SDK/DSA/corporate framing.
- Existing 🔥 style examples remain channel-local and are included in the writer prompt.

## Media
- Audio/TTS URLs such as `.wav` are no longer exposed as `🎬 Відео`.

Install into a fresh folder and copy the complete existing `Data` directory. Do not overlay runtime files.
