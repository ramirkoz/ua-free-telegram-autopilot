# Autopilot 2.2.55: factual source-to-final validation

Four conservative source-grounded checks from the operator's v2.2.54 editorial audit:

1. Distinguish city of residence from event location; a train route cannot establish the incident address.
2. Do not turn an old, explicitly non-current video into a new incident report.
3. Preserve percent growth **by** a percentage rather than turning it into **to** a percentage.
4. Do not invent category superlatives contradicting the source's numerical breakdown.

The checks run before AI outputs are approved and again before publication, including previously prepared content. Source-guided writing prompts also stress numeric scope, research limitations and dated service notices. Tests cover the four observed errors and safe counterexamples.

Does not perform general semantic entailment or automatically alter older publications. Does not change Data, source configuration, timing, media, AI budgets or historical delivery safeguards. Publication acceptance requires CI, Windows Portable/Defender, official release assets, and later operator telemetry.
